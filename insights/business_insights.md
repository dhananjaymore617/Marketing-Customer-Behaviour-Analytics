# Business Insights

This document records findings from the current Power BI analysis. The supplied presentation material is used only as business-case reference; the current Power BI dashboard is the source of truth for the figures documented here.

## Conversion

- Overall conversion rate: **9.6%**.
- Conversion varies materially by month, with the current dashboard showing **17.3% in January** and **6.1% in October**.
- Product-level conversion performance varies considerably, creating opportunities to investigate high- and low-performing products and the customer journey around them.

## Customer engagement

- Total views: **90,79,276**.
- Total clicks: **17,85,010**.
- Total likes: **4,14,122**.
- Clicks represent approximately **19.7% of views** in the current dashboard, while likes represent approximately **4.6% of views**.
- The monthly engagement trend shows declining views over the year, making the timing and content mix of engagement an important area for investigation.

## Customer feedback

- Average customer rating: **3.69**.
- The review analysis includes rating and sentiment dimensions to identify positive, mixed, neutral, and negative customer feedback.
- Review text and sentiment can be used to identify recurring customer pain points and opportunities for product or service improvement.

## Business interpretation

The analysis combines conversion-funnel performance, marketing-content engagement, product performance, and customer feedback so that improvement opportunities can be investigated from multiple parts of the customer experience rather than from a single KPI.
